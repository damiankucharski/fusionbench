# Three rounds on source-only configuration of fusion trees

Can training predictions and training labels identify differences between providers, and can this information lower test loss by selecting an appropriate fusion strategy? We will run three bounded rounds on synthetic data. A configured method deploys a single selected action. All final fits use every source row. Keep mean, Brier-ranked best-prefix Top-N, probability logistic regression (C=1), plain constructive native GP and the historical split-based native-pattern reference as controls. The historical reference uses its own internal selection scheme. Candidates for configuration, in simplicity order, are mean, Top-N, mean with one fitted temperature, derivative-free L2=.25 full-data flat native logit head, and the full-data mean-seeded GP with a training-time distance penalty followed by one fitted temperature (cal_pull, previous study's confirmed policy). Use the preceding study's native budget and fitted-temperature grid. LR is an external comparator.

1. **Measure differences between providers from training data.** Compute disagreement, between-provider Brier spread, sample gain of the best provider over mean, cosine alignment of label residuals, normalized participation ratio of the forecast Gram matrix about the uniform distribution, sample-wise complementarity, mean's least-squares calibration slope measured about uniform, and mean Brier. Report also the standard error for best-minus-mean and the independent unit count. Only the eight named features may enter the learned rule. Study which measurements respond to planted mechanisms on matched data: prespecified directions are larger quality spread for unequal provider quality, less disagreement for correlated errors and greater sample-wise complementarity with a local specialist. Measure paired on/off differences averaged across other switch settings and fraction in the expected direction across seeds. A measured association does not identify a unique mechanism.
   
   Fit a depth-at-most-two cost-sensitive rule offline using the five candidates' *evaluation Brier on previously generated synthetic banks* as training targets and source fingerprints as features. Train on 24 of 30 standard-v6 families with equal family weight. Exclude the three new scene presets and cloned_shortcut, parity and similar_classes_38. Exclude all factorial and continuity banks. At a node test a split after the weighted 20th/40th/60th/80th percentile of a feature, halfway to the next distinct value. Require each side to retain one eighth of the node weight and at least four contributing training families, and local mean Brier improvement at least .001. Ties favor declared feature and action order, then the lower threshold. Lock the learned tree and the best constant action before applying them as policies in round 2. Do not supply family, seed, provider names, class count, provider count, hidden flags or audit outcomes to the prediction rule.
2. **Compare two ways to configure a new fit.** Apply that same frozen tree to the round's source fingerprints. The second method compares the five candidates by three-fold source-only predictions, keeping training scenes together where known. For ungrouped rows stratify when feasible. Among methods within one paired standard error of the empirical OOF winner, take the first in the stated simplicity order. Use seed 3107 for the fold assignment. Refit the returned action on all source data. Paired errors are computed over independent scenes if available. The threshold is a heuristic for selecting complexity. Compare both methods to the constant action learned only from round 1 and to all cheap controls. Choose the method with the lower observed Brier averaged across the 24 allowed families, preferring the fingerprint if tied. The excluded six and all secondary controls never participate in policy training or algorithm selection. Freeze this decision before round 3.
3. **Confirm on new banks and measure what cannot be inferred.** Primary endpoint is chosen rule minus the frozen constant policy in observed full-class Brier across all 30 fixed scenarios: upper 95% paired endpoint below zero. For a claim of transfer across scenario families, also require an upper endpoint below zero on the excluded six and at most +.005 against the constant on the earlier scene16 scarcity task. Report mean risk for the best test action per bank as a diagnostic bound, the loss of matching the same action frequencies to banks without using their characteristics, and program/provider costs. For a separate indistinguishability witness, give a policy the exact same source labels and predictions in two different target worlds. One world's true class probability equals a single provider and the other's equals the arithmetic mean. Under squared loss any shared prediction pays average excess Brier of at least half the mean squared difference between those two probabilities. This diagnostic is outside the confirmatory panel.

For round r use base=610000+1000r. Standard-v6 uses all 30 families, 512 fit and 512 audit observations, 4/3/6 fresh seeds from base+400 in rounds 1/2/3 respectively. Separately, retain the eight matched combinations of provider quality, shared residual and local specialization at ordinary-provider signal slope 1.8: draw 8/8/16 seeds from base, 16 source scenes (128 rows), 256 independent target scenes (2,048 rows), fixed across the eight cells within a seed, and randomize anonymous provider order per seed identically across cells and splits. At base+100 and +200 also retain the earlier scene16, scene128, weak and strong controls with 8/8/16 draws. In round 3 the two target worlds share 16 additional source draws from base+800, with 128 source and 2,048 audit rows, three independent provider probabilities uniform on [.05,.95]. The source label law uses the first provider. Total 216, 186 and 404 conditions respectively. Plot or tabulate all 30 families per round; summarize the earlier 27, the training 24 and the omitted six. Average factorial cell changes within each seed before resampling; standard-panel comparisons resample seeds within each fixed family, 10,000 paired draws at seed 73000+r. Report macro-F1 and binary AP as secondary outcomes. The optimizer seed is fixed. Separate labelled test-oracle decisions from all deployable rules; no real datasets or further experiments in this series.
