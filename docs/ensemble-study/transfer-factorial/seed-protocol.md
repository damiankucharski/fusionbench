# Secondary check of training-bank and optimizer variation

Freeze this secondary design before reading the stratified-study result tables. Use the public StratifiedPool with weak specialization 0.25 and the same population at fit and evaluation. For each new seed 257100–257115, call scenario.sample(512, default_rng([seed, 1])). As in stratified.py, take the first 32 sampled observations of each (amplitude, observed label) stratum in their original order, then restore their combined input order. Each bank has 128 fit rows and is shared by every method and optimizer seed.

Fit ordinary RegularizedNativePatternOKAPI for each combination of the 16 banks and six optimizer seeds 0–5, always with split_seed=0. Fit arithmetic mean, Brier-ranked prefix Top-N and probability logistic stacking (C=1) once per bank. Run one single-threaded CPU worker. All models receive only the fit observations and labels. Save their empirical full-class Brier, chosen family and positive probability on each of the 512 population states. Evaluate exact population Brier, Bayes Brier and excess by integrating against the known state masses and posterior q.

At each state write the 16-by-6 table of forecasts as p(b,s). With uniform weights on those banks and seeds, partition mean squared error against q into (i) the squared difference between the grand forecast mean and q, (ii) the variance across banks of their six-seed forecast means, and (iii) mean within-bank variance across the six optimizer seeds. Multiply each term by two and integrate over the population. Use population variances with divisor 16 or 6. For each reference method use its one fitted forecast per bank with zero within-bank optimizer variance. Check that the three terms sum exactly, up to floating-point error, to mean full-class Brier excess. The within-bank term includes bank-by-optimizer interaction. These components describe this finite crossed grid.

Compare regularized OKAPI with arithmetic mean using the per-bank average of six separately evaluated risks minus the mean method's risk. Report its mean, both ends of a two-sided paired bootstrap percentile interval from 10,000 draws of the 16 banks using default_rng(97946), and bank-level directions with tolerance 1e−12. Keep the six optimizer seeds fixed in the bootstrap. Also report the contrasts and variance partition for the three references, the Bayes floor and the signed fit-to-population gap. This secondary check does not change the original study's protocol or ranking.
