# Does changing an existing operator family help more than changing one operand?

## Question and fixed comparison

The prior singleton-append pilot varied the operator and the provider supplied by a new branch; varying providers improved the chance of finding a better immediate proposal. The present experiment changes an existing operation inside an actual evolving offspring and measures its descendants under an equal fitness budget. Langdon, Barrett and Buxton used arity-preserving point mutation in GP-based classifier fusion as part of a mixture of operators. Their result neither establishes an effect of standalone relabeling nor controls for an operand change. Use independent data banks 4200–4211 and native GP seeds 331 and 337. Train each on both of the laws below. All arms receive the same six anonymous provider probability columns and observed binary fit labels. No operand roles, formulas, conditional probabilities or labels on validation/test rows are available to proposal or survival. All source data are synthetic.

At each native generation first perform the ordinary population crossover, structural and parameter mutations. From the full ordinary offspring pool immediately before native structural deduplication, uniformly choose **one eligible tree** having an operator with at least one direct child, then uniformly choose one such operator in that tree, then uniformly choose one of its direct child slots. If there are no eligible offspring this generation, perform no intervention. Uniformly propose another of the five operator families and draw its native parameters on the same existing operands. Separately uniformly propose any different provider identity for the chosen child. A child can carry its own updates: change only its raw input provider and preserve all descendants. Keep the parent anchor and other ordered children. A replacement of the operator family preserves its full arity, input identity and the old downstream program. A newly selected weighted mean or threshold starts with parameters from its ordinary constructor. Precompute both factor choices under independent local per-generation random streams in every arm, without reading fit labels, so simulating an unused alternative never advances ordinary GP's random stream. Treat the plain control as a genuine native trace with unchanged candidates. All four arms use the same event policy. The per-run event stream starts from the first uint32 word of SeedSequence([data_seed,gp_seed,419]); the generation number, tree, operator and child choice are logged. Position selection precedes family and provider choice. The generator samples a row mask, a complete corner-bit matrix, a complete interior tick matrix and the label uniforms in this order. Once trajectories diverge, eligible families, gates and parents can differ.

On the chosen offspring independently turn type replacement and provider replacement on/off, giving unmodified ordinary GP, type only, provider only, and both. Replace the candidate in its original slot; never add four counterfactuals to the same population. Use native population size six, population multiplier two, tournament two, unrounded scalar positive-class Brier for archive selection, three-decimal fitness for stock population survival, the six ordinary operators MEAN, MIN, MAX, WEIGHTED_MEAN, FAR, CLOSE and no survivor-fitness reuse. Log opportunity, actual candidate evaluation and survival of the exact edited object, including duplicate rejection; counts of entire generations expose variation in the number of opportunities at equal objective cost. All campaigns use the same seed list regardless of realized edits. No second source of labels, fits or evaluation credits enters a proposal. A neutral operation may still have a different computational cost.

## Generator and a limit for one operation

For each draw, six anonymous columns are conditionally independent given a row type. With probability 1/2 draw six independent fair corner bits coded by low 1/64 and high 63/64. Otherwise draw six independent uniform integer ticks in 1 through 63 and divide by 64. For each bank, randomly assign latent roles A, B, C, D using NumPy SeedSequence([data_seed,229]); the other two columns are nuisance. The composition law is q=max(min(A,B),min(C,D)). The logistic control is q=sigmoid(0.2 + 0.7 logit(A) + 0.7 logit(B)). Use a common uniform for generating the two labels. The stock composition witness implements the target with two MIN operations and one MAX, without any optimized parameters. It is an independently scored reference and never seeds the learner.

The six-column corner subpopulation gives an exact witness against every single ordinary operator of arbitrary arity, with any repeated raw input IDs. On a binary-coded corner, CLOSE and FAR reduce to MIN, MAX, or the first argument in a tie. Check all nonempty subsets for MIN/MAX and every projection. For a weighted mean over nonnegative weights summing to one, symmetry or convexity gives the best possible average at equal masses on the four signal coordinates and zero on the other two. In bit units the minimum excess is 1/8 (MIN 3/16, MAX 5/16). In probability units this gives exactly 961/8192 as the best one-operation excess on the complete corner cube; on the whole mixed IID distribution every such candidate therefore incurs at least 961/16384. Report a cube enumeration and a genuine three-operation OKAPI witness. This argument gives no positive lower bound on two-operation trees and does not include a learned affine-logistic head in the one-operation family.

Generate 512 fit, 256 validation, 8192 IID, and 8192 interior-only shift rows for each bank with independent split seeds [data_seed,split_id] for split IDs 0–3, respectively. Evaluate all frozen winners on both laws on all test features; report observed Brier and known conditional excess separately. For each fit law learn the all-provider arithmetic mean, one ranking of individual providers by fit Brier and selection over every prefix length 1–6, and regularized logistic stacking on the exact same inputs and labels. Save each baseline's held-out scores, complete Top-N prefix scores and chosen N. Use no genealogy information for selection.

## Endpoints

Primary is paired **type-only minus provider-only conditional excess** at 1800 native training fitness evaluations, for the fit-archive champion on the matched IID composition law. A negative difference supports the type replacement advantage under this defined budget; a positive one supports changing the operand; an interval spanning zero leaves their ordering unresolved. Also compare each factorial cell to unchanged GP, report the interaction (both−type−provider+stock), and test whether the upper bank-level interval for its IID excess falls below the analytic single-operation bound. Such a result certifies predictive performance beyond every one-update model in the stated family, without implying global search optimality. Average starts within the 12 banks; use two-sided t intervals over paired bank differences. Treat all other contrasts as descriptive. Retain all runs even if no eligible event occurs or an edited child disappears before fitness evaluation.

Repeat summaries at exactly 600 native evaluations and after 100 complete ordinary generations, including their fitness costs; record both fit-selected and independently validation-selected winners, original programs and sizes. Compare stacked baseline and native arms on the logistic control and the two mechanism shifts independently. The outcome may favor the unmodified process or differ by generator. An independent counter checks untouched stock trajectories and an independently implemented interpreter evaluates saved programs on fixed examples before publication.
