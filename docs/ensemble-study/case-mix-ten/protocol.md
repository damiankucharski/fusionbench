# Can case-mix weighting during native tree search improve transfer?

Manual ten-round synthetic study. The last study showed that the mixed source tied the reliability of a shortcut to the prevalence of cases a stable specialist handles. The previous population calculation predicts a maximum regret of 0.04194 for a Bayes forecast on the standardized equal mixture, versus the unrestricted minimax-regret bound 0.03568. The preregistered test asks how much of this is attainable in finite samples by changing the training fitness of an actual native tree.

## Training and controls

Use complete source rows, anonymous provider reports and observed environment E and binary context C. Estimate weights from all source covariates, without outcomes, before the fixed discovery/validation split. For each training row set w=P(E)P(C)/P(E,C) from the observed counts. Each native fitting stage normalizes its own subset of these weights. Full weighting compares providers' joint predictions by weighted full-class Brier during GP evolution and shortlisting, native heads and their feature screen, GP and family validation, and final native head refitting. Keep native GP's unit fitness/tournament rounding, variance-scaled L2 regularizer, seed zero, split seed zero and eight discovery finalists. Internal penalization and operator library are fixed. The two stage ablations use the identical native routine with different stage weights. All rows, training labels and fit budgets are matched.

Use a minimum of four observed rows in every environment by context cell. If this fails, or a factor has only one level, retain ordinary uniform weights. Normalize clipped weights to mean one after upper clipping. Compare ordinary regularized native-pattern OKAPI, arithmetic mean, train Brier-ranked best prefix Top-N and probability logistic stacking (C=1) in every round. `full` uses unrestricted weights, `cap2` clips at 2, `guarded` uses full if the row-weight effective sample fraction is at least .65, otherwise cap2 if its fraction is at least .65, otherwise native. The effective count is (sum w)^2/sum(w²). This measures weight concentration and treats rows as independent. Report grouped standard scenarios separately, with true scene IDs retained for auditing. No additional model or environment metadata are used at prediction.

Every round runs the complete 30-family standard-v6 panel with 512 training and 512 test rows. Actual acquisition-environment labels are unavailable for these scenarios. For this stress test form two environments from provider disagreement above its source median and two contexts from consensus confidence above its median. Compute the labels on all training rows, including correlated scenes. These proxies depend on the reports. In the focal source designs there really are two observed generating environments and a region cue. All focal targets enumerate the same 16 anonymous report patterns, with activity 0, 0.5 and 1 at region prevalence 0.5, and activity .5 at region prevalence .8. Only the audit sees the target posteriors and roles. Also fit a diagnostic source frequency table using the same focal rows, Jeffreys smoothing (half a positive, half a negative per observed report pattern), and uniform/full/cap2 weights, before looking at target risks. This finite-support control measures whether within-pattern estimation responds to the correction.

## Ten experimental comparisons

| Round | Design and falsifiable comparison |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Does full or capped weighting improve mixed worst-endpoint regret vs ordinary native? Case-mix-only and the 30 standard cases test collateral loss. |
| 2 | Permute source environment IDs (seed,1042) before constructing full weights. Compare with the same source rows and genuine IDs; add varied shortcut activity with constant region proportions. |
| 3 | Reverse the association of region prevalence with shortcut activity. Compare full weighting and weighting the discovery stage alone, including the GP path. |
| 4 | Keep discovery unweighted and apply full weights only to validation selection, or only to full-data refitting. Contrast with full and ordinary training. |
| 5 | Stress lack of overlap with source region rates .035/.965 at activity 0/1. Compare weight clipping at 1.5, at 2, and no clipping. |
| 6 | Reduce the mixed source training count from 512 to 128, keeping exact target laws unchanged. Track cell support, effective counts and fallbacks. |
| 7 | Flip the observed context metadata with probability .25, keeping all provider reports incl. the region cue and all labels fixed. Does partial information support the same correction? |
| 8 | Shuffle full-source weights across rows (seed,1048), preserving weight histogram and effective N. Include a homogeneous source with no systematic change in either mechanism. |
| 9 | Choose among guarded, cap2 and full by lowest mixed mean per-source maximum endpoint regret, after requiring empirical standard delta vs native at most +.005 and case-mix/specialist80 delta at most +.010. Fall back to native if none qualify. Exact tie priority guarded, cap2, full. Commit selection and source-score hash before R10 starts. |
| 10 | Independent confirmation with all 30 standard families and eight focal source designs. Primary criterion upper paired 95% limit <0 on mixed/max_regret versus native. Require upper limits <=+.005 on standard, <=+.010 on case_mix/specialist80 and <=+.010 on mixed/s0. Stop after this round. |

For round r use 1040000+1000r plus 400 for standard or 100 for focal, with consecutive independent seeds. R1–8/R9/R10 use 2/3/6 fresh draws per standard family and 6/10/20 per focal profile. Always include mixed and case_mix. Add varied R2/10, reversed R3/9/10, extreme R5/10, small128 R6/10, noisy_context R7/10, homogeneous R8/10. Source profiles sharing a seed are matched interventions, not independent draws. Test performance uses full-class Brier and macro-F1. For each focal fit, subtract each endpoint's own exact Bayes Brier from its score, take the larger difference, then average across source draws. Aggregate standard risks weight all 30 fixed families equally. Bootstrap paired source seeds within each family for the standard macro (10,000 draws at 103000+r); the family set is fixed. Also report realized tree changes, deployments, sample support and timing. The experiment tests one native search configuration and synthetic transfers.
