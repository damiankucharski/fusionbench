# Round one, frozen diagnostic

Across 144 banks the discovery winner's mean advantage over arithmetic averaging was 0.169138 Brier on the validation rows used for selection and 0.162226 for exactly the same trees on the independent source audit. Relative optimism was **0.006911** (95% bank-resampling interval 0.002577 to 0.011348). Seventeen of 24 scenarios had positive mean optimism. All 144 winners beat averaging on internal validation; five scored worse than averaging on the source audit. Absolute optimism, defined as source-audit minus validation Brier of that same tree, was 0.006165.

Deployment includes the incumbent's ordinary head refits. Its source-audit, suite-test and provider-swap-test losses were 0.197040, 0.214424 and 0.260717. Those comparisons include domain or prediction changes, whereas the optimism measurement compares the same frozen programs across two source samples. The interval uses 5,000 independent draws of six banks with replacement per scenario (random seed 328456), keeping equal weight on each of the fixed 24 scenarios. The three-worker panel took 30.08 seconds.
