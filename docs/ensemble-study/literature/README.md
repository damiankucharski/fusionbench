# Literature and evidence for the FusionBench paper

This index connects published findings to the design choices they inform and to the evidence collected in FusionBench and OKAPI. The [dated review](2026-11-21.md) records the original questions and the locations of the results in the papers. The citation keys below are ready to use with the [BibTeX bibliography](references.bib).

## Sources and corresponding claims

| Topic and citation keys | What the source establishes | What we can say about FusionBench or OKAPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning a stacker from predictions — `ting1999stacked` | Ting and Witten trained a linear combiner on out-of-fold probabilities. It reduced classification error compared with selecting one classifier on nine of ten datasets, with one tie (Tables 3–4). Their combiner used multi-response linear regression. | We compare regularized logistic stacking on probabilities and logits. On the Wisconsin held-out set the native binary OKAPI head reproduced both fitted mappings on float64 inputs. Adding a maximum did not establish a gain over the flat logit fit. [Wisconsin protocol and scores](../real-affine-head/README.md) · [OKAPI implementation and precision limits](https://github.com/damiankucharski/OKAPI/blob/15fbac6/experiments/affine_heads/README.md) |
| Correlated, calibrated sources — `kahn2004aggregating`, `radchenko2023negative` | Kahn derives signed weights for class-conditional Gaussian log odds with shared covariance (equations 5, 10–12). Radchenko, Vasnev and Wang study the related bias–variance trade-off from constraining weights for point forecasts under squared error (sections 2 and 5). | We specialize Kahn’s model to two marginally calibrated providers. For our parameters the weak source’s optimal weight changes sign at correlation 0.5. At 0.85, signed fusion improved IID Brier by 0.02823 relative to the nonnegative fit. When correlation fell to zero, the same frozen fit worsened Brier by 0.09209. These values and the monotone-family bound are documented in our study. [Derivation and paired results](../calibrated-suppressor/README.md) The shared-covariance calculation also exposed incorrect probability-reference temperatures on both multiclass generators; an independent density calculation and 2,460 replayed result rows verified the correction. [Multiclass derivation and impact](../multiclass-oracle/README.md) |
| Fitting parameters within a structure — `golub1973differentiation`, `kommenda2020parameter` | Golub and Pereyra eliminate linear parameters in a separable least-squares problem. Kommenda et al. found that local constant optimization in symbolic regression often improved test performance across 94 regression tasks, with sensitivity to initialization. | We tested an exact coordinate update for a native CLOSE/FAR threshold. Against continued evolutionary search at matched cost, the paired difference in excess Brier for changing one input and its threshold was −0.000180 (95% interval −0.000558 to +0.000198). Thirteen of 24 starting champions on the switch law contained no threshold. This tests a discrete gate and a different objective. [Protocol, controls and results](../search-regret/native-refinement/README.md) A follow-up introduced a fitted gate at one terminal and allowed it to enter the breeding population. On an anonymous FAR-switch generator its test excess Brier was 0.00539, versus 0.01545 for candidate-cost-matched GP and 0.01791 if the same gate stayed only in the archive. The stationary logistic control left the matched-GP advantage unresolved. [Gate insertion and transfer controls](../search-regret/native-graft/README.md) |
| Optimism from selecting on validation — `cawley2010overfitting` | Cawley and Talbot demonstrate that noisy model selection can bias the reported error. Their nested and non-nested kernel-regression evaluations illustrate the effect (Table 8). | We chose among 1, 4 or 12 frozen OKAPI trees. At 64 validation labels per bank the gain from 12 over 1 was 0.00738 Brier on the selection set, 0.00359 for the same winners on an independent validation set, and 0.00288 on an independent IID test. Sixteen new banks and a duplicate-tree control separated selection optimism from genuine search gain. [Protocol, intervals and per-bank choices](../validation-optimism/README.md) In the matched four-input lattice, exact fit recovered the generating posterior on 12/12 banks with 256 labels, while native GP missed it in 24/24 starts at both three-decimal and unrounded fitness. Here the dominant loss was search, separately measurable from selection noise. [Precision and exact fit/population comparisons](../selection-precision/README.md) |
| Input-dependent expert selection — `jacobs1991adaptive` | Jacobs et al. trained a gate with local experts. On one vowel-recognition task, their mixtures and monolithic controls reached the same reported test accuracy, with different training costs (Table 1). | Our GatedExperts generator supplies a noisy selector for a latent region. NativeSwitch instead chooses a provider by its distance from a threshold. The latter is our controlled construction for testing whether search can recover a known rule. [GatedExperts generator](../../../fusionbench/scenarios/mixture.py) · [NativeSwitch experiment and linear control](../search-regret/native-switch/README.md) |
| Calibration versus complementary decisions — `rahaman2021uncertainty` | Rahaman and Thiéry show that averaging changes confidence and can worsen calibration when members are already underconfident. Positive temperature scaling after averaging preserves the winning class (Figure 1 and section 4). | Our six-pattern example holds every arithmetic mean at 0.5 while varying which pair of providers is confident. It gives an analytic counterexample to recalibrating the mean. The review proposes a separate empirical test of whether evolutionary search can find the rule. [Construction and proposed control](2026-11-21.md#trzy-hipotezy-do-rozstrzygnięcia) |
| Identifying equivalent trees — `beadle2008semantic` | Beadle and Johnson use binary decision diagrams to test semantic equivalence between a child and a parent during Boolean crossover. | Our MIN/MAX experiment kept one representative per function in a population. The primary result at 300 calls was inconclusive. At 1,200 calls the population explored 113 distinct functions against 51 for structural deduplication. [Intervention, budget controls and held-out results](../search-regret/functional-key/README.md) On fresh FAR-switch banks, forcing a functionally neutral gate into the same survivor slot outperformed matched reordering by 0.00334 excess Brier, and restoring its fitted operand brought a further 0.00288 improvement. Neutrality applied at insertion; descendants could use the operation differently. [Matched-state study](../search-regret/matched-graft/README.md) A frozen-population audit found zero productive native crossover opportunities in 32 matched G15 populations. All 25,600 scored programs across two laws were equivalent to raw-input chains. [Native opportunity and structural classification](../semantic-donors/README.md) On eight inputs, matched branches reached the desired two-pathway function in 21/24 runs against 11/24 for chains, despite fewer distinct archived functions on average. [Semantic coverage under matched initialization](../seed-geometry/README.md) |
| Point mutation of an existing function — `langdon2002combining`, `poli2008fieldguide` | In a GP experiment combining neural networks, Langdon, Barrett and Buxton randomly replaced a function with one of the same arity. Its argument subtrees stayed in place. On average, 10% of the functions in a program undergoing point mutation were changed. The experiment used several genetic operators together. Poli, Langdon and McPhee give the general definition and specify that a node with no alternative of the same arity stays unchanged. [Primary study, §5.5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4471-0123-9_51) · [Field Guide, §2.4, p. 16](https://digitalcommons.morris.umn.edu/cs_facpubs/1/) | The FusionBench pilot appended an operator and a companion provider to a singleton. Across 24 banks, redrawing companions with the same sequence of six operator proposals improved fit-selected IID excess Brier by 0.00956 (95% paired interval 0.00645–0.01266) relative to a fixed companion. An OKAPI test of point mutation requires an existing function node and preserves its ordered operands when changing its operator. The pilot measured the effect of companion choice. [Pilot design and results](../operator-coverage/README.md) A subsequent experiment changed the type at an existing node or the ID of one of its direct operands. At 1800 native evaluations the composition-law type-minus-provider excess was −0.00302 (95% interval −0.00923 to +0.00320) across 12 independent data banks. [Organic factorial comparison](../search-regret/organic-composition/README.md) The subsequent deployable-flag regression panel improved mean IID full Brier from 0.26839 to 0.25947 across all 19 defaults, with 15 favorable point estimates. The individual study does not isolate how adding the option changed competing mutation frequencies. [Canonical scenario panel](../full-suite-point/README.md) A prospective fourth-slot identity control on six new banks isolated the retype effect: IID full Brier −0.01202 [−0.01756, −0.00648] across 19 scenarios, with a −0.00157 difference on an external Wine dataset. Resampling the old family often encountered a parameter-free operation. [Opportunity-matched study](../mutation-opportunity/README.md) |
| Temperature calibration — `guo2017calibration` | Guo et al. fit one positive temperature on held-out logits of a classifier by negative log likelihood. Its softmax class decision is preserved; Table 1 evaluates calibration error on vision and NLP tasks. [Sections 4.2–4.3 and Table 1](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html) | OKAPI fit one temperature on pooled anonymous multiclass probabilities. With 128 labels it improved pooled IID population excess Brier by 0.17167 versus a per-provider CLR logistic stack on the exchangeable cases, and lost by 0.21073 under unequal provider strength. [Protocol and exact scope](https://github.com/damiankucharski/OKAPI/blob/9d52a2a/experiments/temperature_pool/protocol.md) · [Measured result](https://github.com/damiankucharski/OKAPI/blob/118badc/experiments/temperature_pool/README.md) |
| Paid labels for comparing fixed predictors — `hansen1943finite`, `sawade2012active`, `kossen2021active` | Sawade et al. proposed gap-proportional sampling as a wide-Gaussian limiting case under squared loss. Hansen–Hurwitz weighting applies to sampling with replacement. Kossen et al. study bias correction in active model evaluation. | For centered binary Brier differences the same proposal minimizes variance for each fixed label vector. At 32 draws under stable conditional labels, independent-test pair regret was 0.00053 under gap sampling versus 0.00113 under uniform sampling. [Frozen comparisons and limitations](../frozen-pair-ranking/README.md) |

## Recording the next result

For each literature finding used in the project, record the exact claim, its setting and its location in the primary source (page, table, figure or equation) in a dated review. Add its stable DOI or proceedings URL to the bibliography. Use the same citation key in this index and in any scenario docstring or experiment report whose design depends on it. Where a paper provides a related example, identify which assumptions or operations we introduced ourselves.

When an experiment finishes, link the protocol and measured result from the corresponding entry. Keep the dataset or generator, comparator, metric, uncertainty and any failed control with the result. Leave the original dated hypothesis intact so the manuscript can cite the sequence from proposal to test.
