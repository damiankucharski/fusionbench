# Round 2. Requiring improvement across several parts of the fit data

Fix the intervention and evidence criteria before the first round's outcomes. Perform the second round on **new seeds**, with no tuning between rounds. Use the same generator, A-only checkpoint selection, audit, binary references and seeded GP budget as round one.

Choose an anchor on the fit data before evolution: the lowest unrounded full-class Brier among the actual initial population (up to eight provider singletons and the all-provider mean), breaking ties by fewer nodes then initial order. Assign each independent fit group wholly to one of four folds by permuting sorted unique group IDs with default_rng(data_seed + 3,000,000) and distributing them round-robin. Scene IDs are groups in the staged benchmark. Individual examples are groups in the standard suite, which lacks general scene IDs. In these experiments groups within a fit have equal size. All methods receive the same fit data. There is no extra development block.

For a tree, let g_j be its fit-Brier improvement over the frozen anchor in fold j. The proposed fitness uses the second smallest of the four gains, the gain reached in at least three folds. The same score in native units is 1 − (anchor pooled Brier − second_smallest(g))/2; keep the existing size criterion and rounding used during survival. The other path uses ordinary pooled Brier through evolution. Within each path consider two fit-only final decisions, pooled Brier and the fold score, both unrounded and both with the anchor explicitly available. Prefer the anchor on ties, then the smaller tree, then survivor order. Also report the original unprotected pooled path with plain pooled terminal selection and the initial anchor alone. The policy deploys one tree and uses only its selected inputs at inference. The fold statistic is an empirical selection heuristic on repeatedly queried cases. It is not an independent holdout or a confidence certificate.

Use seeds 287200–287231, two laws, and three fit designs (share 0, 24×8; share 1, 24×8; share 1, 192×8). Generate all real searches twice per cell, once per fitness, sharing folds, rows, founders, seed and generation budget. Reuse each fitted path for its two final policies. Supply audit data only after fitness, candidates and choices are frozen. Record hashes, cost, anchor identity/equivalence where available, sampled fit and fresh Brier plus oracle-integrated audit excess. Repeat the exact policy comparison and the four reference methods on all 25 standard-v4 scenarios with data seeds 287300, 287301, 512 fit and 512 test rows and search seed 0. Use the historical adaptive cap max(31,M+1) on both paths.

The single primary is **fold search / fold deployment minus pooled search / pooled deployment, anchor available to both**, in conditional audit excess Brier under the signal law at share 1 and 24×8. A negative difference favors the proposed policy. The two mixed search/deployment cells separate a terminal decision change from a change in breeding. Report the direct comparison with unchanged unprotected GP, all cheap controls, the equal law, the larger sample and scenario-level regressions. Use 10,000 paired bank bootstrap draws with seed 97825. The suite interval resamples the two paired seeds within each of the 25 fixed families. An interval below zero on the primary supports a scoped improvement. A broadly recommended change would also require independent validation of suite performance and robustness outside the generator.
