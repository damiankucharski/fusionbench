Vary the share s of provider residual variance shared by a scene over {0, 0.5, 1}. In the checkpoint generator, coefficients on static shared/row error are sqrt(0.5 s), sqrt(0.5 (1−s)); coefficients on the two AR(1) checkpoint paths are sqrt(0.9725 s), sqrt(0.9725 (1−s)). No perfect provider noise is added on the signal law.

Use those slots throughout. The four methods used as references are mean, fit-Brier ranked best-prefix Top-N, standardized probability logistic stacking, and fixed regularized native-pattern OKAPI.
