# Exact label coverage limit under a train/test change of direction distribution

Round start 2026-11-21 00:10:56 UTC. Freeze two equally probable worlds sharing two noiseless votes and the unlabeled input law. Their third vote is v versus cos(theta)v+sin(theta)w. During training the v-coordinate has a fair sign times an independent chi(k) magnitude; other orthogonal coordinates are independent standard Gaussians. At test all coordinates are independent standard Gaussians. The learner knows both candidate rules and their priors but only final training labels can distinguish them. Derive p_train and p_test for disagreement, the exact Bayes risk of identifying the world and of predicting a test label under 0/1 and binary Brier losses, and a shared four-vote network implementing the posterior prediction. Include the hard-world selection baseline. Use the formula for all n, record numeric values at 0, 32, 128, 512, 2048 and 8192 labels and labels required for 95% chance of witnessing a disagreement.

Direct simulation uses chi degrees [1,7,15,31], theta in [pi/4,pi/3], nested label sizes [32,128,512], 24 independent banks seeded 1250–1273 with 64 fresh training experiments and 256 test rows per experiment per cell. Sample actual raw coordinates and final labels, not indicators drawn directly from the theoretical probability. Check calibration of the analytic risks against Monte Carlo and exact shared-network output versus the Bayesian ensemble of the two candidate rules. Main cell is theta=pi/4, k=15, n=512. No fitting across or selection among held-out queries.
