## Matched intervention

Keep singletons and then score 30 additional initial candidates, including duplicates, per arm. STOCK uses the public adapter's usual random subset shallow seeds. In the two controlled arms draw the same 30 plans with NumPy default_rng(search_seed), fresh per arm. Choose four ordered provider IDs A,B,C,D uniformly without replacement. If fewer than four exist, include each at least once, add uniform draws with replacement to reach four, and shuffle. Draw a fair inner MIN/MAX, with the other as outer. BRANCH computes outer(inner(A,B),inner(C,D)). CHAIN computes inner(outer(inner(A,B),C),D). Both use the same ordered signal occurrences and same three operator families/multiplicities in seven native nodes. They differ in topology and the repertoire of functions it generates. The adapter's actual parent pool, tournament, rounded selection, node-count criterion, point mutation switched off, training and selection data and raw evaluation limits are shared within each panel. The ordinary initializer is a practical reference and also changes the node/op counts relative to the two interventions. Use native OKAPI 3eae0e5480cba0471cc5fda71b0c90f35b6a73f2. Native parents, offspring, survivors and ordinary fitness calls are real throughout.

A four-signal universe has six distinct BRANCH functions and 24 CHAIN functions in these templates. For M at least four, a single random seed has probability 1/[6 choose(M,4)] of representing a fixed member of the BRANCH family, or 1/[24 choose(M,4)] for the CHAIN family. The factorials include permutation symmetries. Report the probabilities for 30 independent draws and actual seed coverage. Random starts use two fixed plan streams, so measured frequencies across tasks with the same true providers are dependent. Distinguish target inclusion during initialization, first emergence after initialization, final selection and fit improvement by ordinary evolution. The composition rule is deliberately placed inside a candidate template. This tests a search bias and its price under mismatched rules.

## Follow-up decision

Archive the study even for a null or reversed result. After this intervention, compare ordinary validation to the existing validation-by-context mixture selector on one frozen candidate pool per bank. Test shifts in the frequencies of known contexts separately from shifts in the label mechanism at unchanged observable input marginals, with the same three baselines. The earlier one-bit observer estimates mixture weights from unlabelled disagreement and groupwise labeled validation losses; a change in the unknown label mechanism need not change that disagreement. This is a separate prospective trial. [Older observer and mixture selector, FusionBench 1dfb93e](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/1dfb93e/fusionbench/eval/observer.py)

The initialization comparison is our hypothesis. Beadle and Johnson's 2008 Boolean GP experiment tested rejecting semantically equivalent children in crossover. Here exact semantic equality is a measurement, without rejection of a candidate during initialization.
