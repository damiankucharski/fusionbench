# Can two individually calibrated providers require subtraction?

Freeze this study before computing scores. It diagnoses a representational limit of the nonnegative weighted logit pool used in OKAPI. The study tests the operator family. It makes no claim about the full GP palette.

Draw balanced binary labels in expectation. Given the class, draw two Gaussian source logits with standard deviations 2 and 1, means equal to ± half their respective variances, and conditional correlation ρ. Each separate output is its true marginal posterior. Compare ρ = 0, 0.5 and 0.85. The Gaussian likelihood ratio predicts that the optimal coefficient on the weaker source changes sign at 0.5. The stronger source alone is a feasible tree. With a negative true second coefficient it is also the minimum population Brier predictor among **all** predictors nondecreasing in the weaker source, conditional on the stronger one. Derive that bound and verify the oracle against a class-conditional likelihood calculation.

Use 24 independent data seeds 41000–41023. Per correlation and seed, fit on 384 rows, report 256 independent validation rows, 8192 IID and 8192 shifted rows. Shift 0.85 to 0 and the other two correlations to 0.85. Freeze the fit before generating held-out rows. Randomize provider column order per bank for all methods, keep it fixed across splits. Provider identity and generation parameters are available only to the analytic references. Compare arithmetic mean, full ranked-prefix Top-N fit by Brier, both FusionBench C=1 stackers (raw probabilities and logits), the strong source, and the analytic Bayes posterior for each test law. The strong-source control uses the population identity. Fit an extra binary logit stacker with nonnegative effective slopes, a free intercept and the same L2 objective as the unrestricted stacker. Record coefficients, timing, positive-class Brier, fit, validation, IID and shift losses. Report the paired mean difference in IID Brier between the two fitted logit arms at 0.85 and its 95% t interval over 24 banks. Control predictions are near-ties between fitted logit arms at ρ=0 and larger error for the frozen signed predictor on shifting 0.85 to 0. Treat these as secondary. Repeat a bank exactly and check sign-change and monotonicity cases on a numerical reference.
