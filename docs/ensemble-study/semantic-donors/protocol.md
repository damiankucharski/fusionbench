# Can two useful intermediate programs meet during native crossover?

Frozen on 2026-11-21 before any new panel. On the earlier graded four-provider challenge the exact empirical minimizer recovered the true two-pathway law on all twelve 256-label banks, while the four-singleton native search missed it in every one of 24 starts after 400 evaluations at each precision. This follow-up measures whether the right parents or subtrees ever coexist, whether actual tournaments can select them, and whether a standard swap of two sites can produce the target. It observes ordinary GP; it introduces no new search policy to the default regression battery. [Prior search and exact-population result](../selection-precision/README.md)

## Populations, inputs and targets

Reuse the exact 166 nonconstant MIN/MAX functions of four variables evaluated on all 256 four-level atoms. Keep the earlier matched posterior max(min(A,B), min(C,D)), logit-additive control, atom distributions and uniform random permutation of the named roles. Use 16 independent new banks 6600–6615, 256 hard fit labels, independent 256 validation and 2048 each IID and predefined shift, with GP starts 809 and 821. Standard native MIN/MAX GP starts from the four singleton inputs, keeps four parent trees, population multiplier two, tournament size two, stock three-decimal selection, no size/depth caps and no retype. Use OKAPI 3eae0e5. The raw fitness is the usual one minus positive-channel Brier, using integer counts to tie genuinely equal empirical losses exactly. Every training fitness call counts, including repeated programs. The primary archived search checkpoint is 400 real evaluations, with unrounded fit selection, val/fit tie selection and stable first occurrence. Report exact fit regret, empirical optimum hits, exact population IID and shifted risk, plus the three matched baselines. Fit the mean of all providers, Top-N with a single Brier ranking and all prefixes, and regularized logistic stacking on the same labeled rows. Freeze before validation and test. The raw anonymous providers and fit labels are all that search sees.

The primary analysis-only target is the true matched lattice rule. The additive posterior lies outside this grammar. For its population target use the first minimum-IID-risk lattice rule in the same canonical enumeration and specify any equal-risk group. Record all exact empirical fit optima as a separate diagnostic on both laws. Inference about the known matched target does not rely on which fit optimum occurs in a bank.

## Exact opportunity in a frozen live population

Observe the native survivor population **at the beginning of generations 1, 5, 10 and 15**, after initial fitness and immediately before the first crossover proposal. Keep tracing actual generation stages until 400 fitness calls have been archived. If 400 would be reached earlier, continue only as far as required to observe G15, and mark costs and outcomes beyond the archived snapshot separately. Preserve native RNG, object order and mutation behavior. Archive actual candidates in the order they arose after crossover, after possible additional mutations, after cohort deduplication when scored, and among final survivors. A semantically identical variant in a later generation alone does not establish ancestry. The traced distribution gives opportunities for the already sampled live parents.

A single uncapped native crossover comprises two independent size-two tournaments, a uniform allowed node class (value always, operator only when both parents have an operator), one uniform eligible site in each parent, and a swap that yields both children. The same individual can win both tournaments. The probability that individual i wins one tournament is (# rivals with worse fitness-distance + 0.5 × # other rivals tied) divided by the number of unordered pairs. Use actual rounded fitness and native distances, including ties. For each ordered pair of parents, every admissible site-class and ordered site pair, compute the semantic functions of both offspring. For an operator site, the incoming accumulated parent value can depend on preceding sibling operators. Replacing an operator must therefore preserve this context. Pure MIN/MAX functions can be evaluated on all 16 Boolean corners by AND and OR; equality on all corners entails equality on all real inputs by thresholding. The use of that algebra changes no native proposal.

The **primary endpoint** is the unconditional probability, at G15, that one ordinary native crossover draws two parents whose root predictions both differ from the true matched rule and produces at least one offspring equal to that rule. Keep every run in the denominator, including populations with no eligible combination or with the target already present. Average the two starts within each of the 16 independent matched banks, then give the mean, full per-start zero counts, range and a paired-bank two-sided 95% t interval. This is a probability conditional on each realized live population, marginalizing the randomized operator draws. It describes the next recombination under the native sampling distribution; selection would still have to retain any useful child.

Secondary contrasts use uniformly drawn parents instead of tournaments with the same eligible node swaps, and uniform viable site draws versus mere combinatorial reachability. Decompose the ladder into target-free eligibility, at least one fruitful node pair, positive probability under actual tournaments, observed births in crossover, possible mutation, scoring and survival. Report analogous G1/G5/G10, the additive control, and actual 400-call archive reach. On each frozen population compute separate hypothetical capabilities: (a) the target is the MIN or MAX of whole roots from two distinct currently present individuals; (b) the target is the MIN or MAX of two Value-rooted subtrees from distinct live parents; (c) the target is the MIN or MAX of two whole programs previously actually scored by that time. Exclude target-containing roots as direct ingredients for a productive creation, and distinguish target-equal ingredient subtrees in the two-subtree test. Combining two whole roots at a new operation differs from a stock subtree swap. A historically seen donor need not be available to the actual GP population.

## Structural references and independent verification

A chain formed from a singleton by repeatedly attaching a raw input with MIN or MAX can only produce a function with a necessary input (last operation MIN) or a sufficient input (last operation MAX). The matched function has neither property. This certificate does not constrain native GP to such chains: it can branch, swap complete subtrees, or copy sites with sequential updates. Enumerate the exact chain closure, the subset of all 166 rules with/without a necessary or sufficient input, and the functions produced by one MIN/MAX join of two chains. Classify actually observed roots against each set. Use these results to distinguish scarcity of intermediate building blocks, low probability of selecting them together, and a missing stock operation on a particular population.

Reconstruct the archived 16-corner traces and independently verify average probabilities, true losses and counts. Cross-check the symbolic swap against native forced sites on nested sequential programs, repeated providers and same-parent selections. Use an independent fresh run without the observer on fixed seeds for trajectory parity. Report the result even when the prospective G15 probability is zero everywhere. These are controlled finite-law diagnostics; any extension suggested by them needs fresh tests and a broad independent regression panel. The plan extends the project's earlier semantic-signature audit. Beadle and Johnson used Boolean decision diagrams to control semantic equality in a distinct GP setting; here truth tables facilitate exact measurement of the offspring distribution on a tiny grammar.
