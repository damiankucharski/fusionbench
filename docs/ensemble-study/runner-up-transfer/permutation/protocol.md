# Paired rowwise class relabelling on existing prediction banks

This is a retrospective mechanism experiment on the previously released heterogeneous digits and directed synthetic panel E. The original method scores are known. The relabelled scores and paired changes have not been measured. The first cohort comprises the eight archived ten-class digits splits with seeds 3140000–3140007. The second comprises the eight ten-class panel E runs with matched fit sizes and seeds 3160000–3160007. Regenerate its fit and test with the published scenario, split sizes, float32 conversion and original RNG streams. No external prediction bank, base learner or prospective forecast is added. [Source study](../../external-coverage/README.md)

For every row independently, draw a uniform ten-class permutation with NumPy default_rng(SeedSequence([original_seed, 330, split])), using split=0 on fit and 1 on test. Entries at new class coordinates receive the corresponding old scores; map y by the inverse permutation. Each row uses the same permutation for every provider. The intervention breaks any persistent class-ID convention across rows and retains the row's probability values, provider-to-provider alignment, true-class probability, and gaps between ordered values. Recompute the maximum discrepancy in the individual true-class probabilities, mean prediction and per-row mean Brier. Tied argmax decisions can change under class reordering.

Use the frozen nine methods and settings from common.evaluate_methods with the original GP seed, applying the same training and test transformations to all methods. The prior raw outputs may be replayed or reused after checking the source and numerical fingerprints. The empirical outcomes include mean, all-prefix Top-N, regularized raw-probability and logit LR, native L2, pooled-full LR, permutation-equivariant LR, temperature and sharpener. Full-class Brier is the main outcome. The main contrast is the change (relabelled minus raw) in native minus raw-probability LR. The secondary contrast is the corresponding change in pooled full minus symmetric LR, which share the same pooled standardization. Report both conditions for each contrast plus the paired change and a two-sided t interval across eight seeds separately for each cohort. Show all nine means and per-seed changes. Digits splits overlap, so its interval describes split variation and is not an independent external replication. Synthetic seeds are independent draws of one fixed directed law.

The arithmetic mean and symmetric LR are invariance controls. Check the mean forecast and conservation of its full Brier to floating-point tolerance; independently fitted symmetric LR must agree in Brier across conditions within 1e−7. If a control fails, investigate before interpreting the comparison. Holding optimizer seeds fixed keeps the stochastic search configuration matched; it does not force identical paths after data change.
