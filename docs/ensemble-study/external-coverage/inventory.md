# What the 19 standard scenarios cover

The version-1 suite contains 17 binary families and two multiclass families. Both multiclass constructors can produce three providers and ten classes. The clearest gap exposed by digits is the distribution of probabilities those providers emit. [Scenario catalog and constructor defaults](../../site/scenarios.md) · [Registry](../../../fusionbench/scenarios/registry.py)

| Families | What the existing settings can vary |
| --- | --- |
| `committee`, `deadweight`, `needle`, `miscalibration` | Binary signal strength, pool size and informative fraction. Miscalibration applies a fixed range of provider-specific logit scales. |
| `correlated`, `calibrated_suppressor`, `polarity_fault` | Redundant evidence, conditional correlation and sign faults. The suppressor has two providers of fixed unequal strengths. |
| `hardveto`, `hardrescue`, `weakest_link`, `two_pathways`, `parity`, `compound`, `gated_experts` | Latent Boolean rules and specialist routing. The knobs change detector strengths, detector counts or replication, depending on the family. |
| `native_switch`, `native_close_switch`, `compositional_switch` | Binary labels depend on discrete provider scores. Knobs change column roles, the label law or the fraction of corner cases. |
| `multiclass_committee`, `saturated` | The former defaults to 8 providers and 5 classes, the latter to 6 providers and 4 classes. Both expose provider count, class count and signal strength. Saturated also controls a common Gaussian noise component. |

The package ships one synthetic archive per family, seed 1729, with 128 fit and 128 test rows. On the saved test sets, mean provider top-1 accuracy was 29.0% for `multiclass_committee` and 90.5% for `saturated`. Both had zero exact-zero probability entries. Their `accuracy` settings of 0.60 and 0.95 control a heuristic boost to the true-class logit. The nine standard generator shifts all concern binary families. ## Where digits differs

Three classifiers learn from the same 898 images, followed by 449 fusion-fit and 450 held-out images. Across eight splits, logistic regression, five-nearest-neighbours and random forest averaged 96.50%, 98.28% and 97.25% top-1 accuracy. Their full-class Brier scores spanned a wider range, respectively 0.07074, 0.02869 and 0.13089. The unweighted neighbour assigns probability in fifths. Every ten-class prediction has at least five exact zeros. The 100-tree forest averages tree probabilities, while logistic regression emits dense probabilities. The eight test splits share images. [Provider recipe](../../../examples/digits_predictions.py) · [Recorded provider losses](../pattern-iterations/digits/providers.csv) · [Split overlap](../pattern-iterations/digits/README.md)

At three providers and ten classes, a high-strength `saturated` configuration can model strong providers with correlated errors. Its label prior is uniform and every provider uses the same strength and Gaussian emission law. For finite logits softmax assigns positive mass to every class, so neither multiclass constructor can generate the neighbour's five-vote support. Their class symmetry also rules out a preset preference for a particular wrong class given the true class. The binary families illustrate unequal skill, discretization and specialization individually. Combining those properties in one multiclass bank calls for different generator code or saved model predictions. Agreement on shapes, top-1 accuracy or error correlation alone leaves the ordering of fusion losses unresolved. ## Real prediction data already in the research archive

- **Wine Recognition** has reusable prediction tensors, labels and row IDs from six providers and three classes. Five outer folds cover all 178 examples once at test. Each fold stores out-of-fold development predictions and separate validation and test predictions. [Arrays](../mutation-opportunity/wine-inputs.npz) · [Provider and partition manifest](../mutation-opportunity/wine-manifest.json)
- **Banknote Authentication, Ionosphere and Spambase** have binary held-out provider probabilities with row and group IDs across five outer folds. These rows come from fold-specific trained providers. [Per-case outputs](../compact-selection/results/predictions.csv.gz) · [Protocol](../compact-selection/protocol.md)
- **Smartphone HAR, Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer and HTRU2** have earlier held-out fusion or compression studies. HAR includes a six-class test on nine held-out people and saved affine expert parameters. Wisconsin and HTRU2 supply binary comparisons. Reproducing the corresponding prediction tensors depends on each study's source data and recipe.
