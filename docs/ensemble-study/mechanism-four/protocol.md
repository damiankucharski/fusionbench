# Synthetic scene mechanisms, compact fusion trees and calibration

## Four rounds and the decision rule

The publication target is a general configurable fusion benchmark. Our application here is to improve full-data constructive OKAPI under small sample and correlated error. This series exclusively uses synthetic observations. It examines plausible mechanisms without assuming a fit to a particular real dataset. Freeze this protocol before scoring.

1. **Does simple averaging retain its advantage as the source of errors changes?** Compare ordinary full-data GP (full), native full-data mean-seeded GP with the training reference penalty from the previous study (pull_mean), the historical split-based native-pattern model (previous8), arithmetic mean (mean), fit-ranked best-prefix Top-N (top_n), logistic stacking on probabilities (lr), and a source-label-free compact mean (sparse_pool). Estimate paired main effects over three controls. As a mechanism check, compute individual and shared provider errors about the known label probability on the same examples. Check the quadratic identity relating average provider loss, diversity and the loss of averaging. The true probabilities and subgroup flags are reserved for scoring.
2. **Can a compact mean seed preserve useful diversity with fewer providers?** Approximate the full mean on *source forecasts* by greedily adding the distinct provider that minimizes the squared error of the equal-weight prefix against the full mean. Choose k=min(M,max(2,ceil(sqrt(M)))). Compare an equally large subset sampled without replacement under fixed RNG seed 421, both as standalone pools and as seeds in native GP. Each GP retains the same all-provider training reference, objective, population quota and budget as pull_mean. Primary comparison sparse_gp minus pull_mean; random_gp and standalone pools separate seed choice from evolution. Report provider counts at deployment.
3. **Is one scalar of probability calibration useful after search?** Keep the source-trained GP tree fixed. Select a temperature multiplier beta=2^(j/4), integer j from −4 through 4, minimizing source full Brier plus (0.25/sqrt(U)) × log2(beta)^2. U is the number of fit scenes if IDs are available or number of fit examples otherwise. Preserve identity and favor it on ties, then smallest absolute j. Apply to pull_mean and sparse_gp (cal_pull and cal_sparse). Also fit an unpenalized calibration of pull_mean (raw_pull) and the same penalized calibration of arithmetic averaging (cal_mean). The calibration and evolution use the same source labels; no independent calibration split. Measure Brier, binary AP and macro-F1. Rounding or clipping can affect ranking ties even when binary predictions are monotone.
4. **Confirm one candidate on fresh synthetic draws.** Eligible candidates in tie order are R2 sparse_gp, R3 cal_pull and R3 cal_sparse. Relative to pull_mean, screen on observed Brier on the entire standard panel (allowed regression .005), earlier scene16 (.005) and strong specialization (.010). Among passing policies select the lowest factorial mean *conditional expected* Brier change. If none pass, first minimize the largest positive violation divided by its permitted threshold. Lock that choice and raw and summary hashes before confirmation. Confirm the primary target when the upper paired 95% endpoint for factorial expected Brier vs pull_mean is strictly negative. A broad recommendation additionally requires the three upper paired endpoints to satisfy their thresholds. Report all four checks and observed-label results. If cal_sparse is chosen also score uncalibrated sparse_gp on the same confirmation banks to isolate the scalar step. The previous split-based model and simple baselines are reference methods throughout.

## Sampling and comparison

Use all 30 ready-made standard-v6 families (the original 27 plus grouped_rare_event, shared_error_bloc and rare_scene_specialist), 512 fit and 512 evaluation rows. Score four controls from the previous study: weak and strong exact stratified-pool laws and the matched rare-event study with 16 scenes ×8 rows or 128 scenes ×1 row. Use a new 2×2×2 factorial with 128 fit rows from 16 scenes and 2,048 audit rows from 256 fresh scenes. Within each data seed keep latent scenes, labels, all underlying noise and diagnostic probabilities paired across cells. The three factors are provider noise heterogeneity (0 or .65), fraction of error shared across providers (0 or .65), and specialist strength (0 or .85, active in 20% of scenes). Event offset 2.0, eight anonymous providers, scene error share .85, rows per scene eight. Training and evaluation have the same data law; there is no imposed drift. The default named scenario also permits these knobs to vary through the public API.

For round r, base is 491000+1000r. First 8 seeds per factorial cell and control for each screen, 24 for confirmation, starting at base for the factorial, base+100 for the old scene controls and base+200 for the stratified laws. Standard seeds start at base+400 (two per family at screening and four at confirmation). Total 156 bank conditions per screening round and 408 in confirmation. Source and audit use disjoint streams. All six reference methods receive the same source rows on a given bank. The native full-data GP has eight generations, one restart, native seed zero, the preceding full-data study's constructive initialization and its structural caps. Only the historical reference has an internal 70/30 partition. The LR penalty parameter is C=1. Top-N ranks providers by source Brier and chooses the best averaging prefix on source labels.

Full-class observed Brier, conditional expected Brier where the synthetic target is known, macro-F1, binary AP, number of tree nodes and distinct providers are reported. On the finite stratified laws Brier is integrated over the support. For factorial conditional Brier integrate out only the Bernoulli label draw, keeping the same evaluation rows and predictions. Use 10,000 paired percentile bootstrap resamples with RNG seed 84100+r. For factorial results average the eight variants *within seed*, then resample seeds. For the fixed standard suite weight the 30 families equally and resample seeds within each family. Per-cell and family comparisons are descriptive and retain their identities. Both training-bank and optimizer variation exist; these experiments vary data seeds at fixed optimizer seed. A simulator stress test does not establish the prevalence of these mechanisms in deployed systems.
