# A matched calibration control on the confirmation banks

After the candidate was frozen and the confirmation runner launched, screening showed that a temperature fitted to the plain arithmetic mean also improved its Brier. Before the confirmation runner wrote its scores, we registered a separate descriptive control: apply exactly the same penalized nine-value grid to the arithmetic mean on all 408 already assigned confirmation banks. Compare its observed and conditional Brier to the confirmed policy, raw mean and logistic stacking, and record unchanged binary ranking/decision metrics. Use the same pairing and family weights as in the primary tables. This control cannot change selection, the four registered confirmation checks, or their status. Replaying it does not introduce new seeds or another experimental round.
