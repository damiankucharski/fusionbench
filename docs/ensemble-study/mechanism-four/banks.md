# What varies within and across a draw

Each factorial draw contains the same 16 training scenes and 256 audit scenes under eight configurations. Each scene contributes eight rows. The fit and audit use independent generator streams. All eight configurations share the scene and row signals, label outcomes, specialist-region membership, and latent probability of an event. Bernoulli events have a marginal prevalence of roughly 10%. Finite banks fluctuate around that prevalence. The simulator marks 20% of scenes as potential specialist scenes in expectation. Some small training draws may have few or none.

| Cell code | Changing from 0 to 1 |
| --- | --- |
| `h` | Set provider-noise heterogeneity to 0.65. Residual standard deviations follow a fixed increasing profile normalized to unit root-mean-square. |
| `c` | Set the common fraction of provider residual variance to 0.65. All providers share a residual component before their individual scaling. |
| `s` | Move provider 3's logit 85% of the way toward the latent event logit within specialist scenes. Predictions outside those scenes and predictions of other providers are identical across this intervention. |

Thus `h1c0s1` describes unequal baseline quality and a regional specialist with independent provider errors conditional on the signal. Every configuration retains the scene-level structure in each provider's errors (85% of residual variance before the specialist adjustment). Region membership is not supplied to the fitter. A clear limitation is that error correlations, quality ordering and the specialist's index persist between training and evaluation. A regime switches independently of the event signal, and the inputs contain no geography, imagery or patient metadata. The roles of actual providers on real data remain to be measured.

The standard panel consists of all 30 standard-v6 families with 512 fit and 512 test rows, including three named scene problems. The grouped rare-event default mixes all three mechanisms; shared_error_bloc gives six providers a common error source while the other two retain independent noise; rare_scene_specialist makes one provider report the true event probability only in its minority regime. Additional controls preserve the previous `scene16` and `scene128` setups in which eight equally competent providers report on the same signal and different allocations of 128 training rows share the audit scenes. The `weak` and `strong` controls use the existing stratified-pool finite-support law at specialization 0.25 and 0.75. These controls are extra paired conditions; the public standard macro weights each of the 30 standard scenarios once.

The first three rounds use 8 new data draws for each factorial cell and each control, and two for each standard family, yielding 156 conditions per round. The fourth uses 24 and four respectively for 408 conditions. For round `r` the seed base is `491000 + 1000*r`. The factorial begins at the base, the two earlier grouped controls at base + 100, the finite-support controls at base + 200, and the standard suite at base + 400. For the factorial, the generators are initialized with `[seed, 0]` for fit and `[seed, 1]` for audit. Within a bank, fit and audit scene IDs occupy disjoint namespaces. The same scene ID in two independent data draws refers only to its position within its own draw.

For the empirical cohorts, Brier, macro-F1 and binary positive-class average precision use the held-out label outcomes. In addition, the grouped generators store the true Bernoulli probability on each audit row. Integrating both possible label outcomes at that probability removes test-label noise while retaining sampling variation in the fit and test scenes. This quantity is conditional on those sampled scenes. It is available to the scorer; fitted methods only receive training inputs and training labels. For weak and strong specialization the finite 512-point support, its exact masses and both label outcomes give population metrics without a sampled audit label. AP remains undefined in these reports for multiclass standard families. [Bank generator and scorer](banks_study.py) · [Tests](test_banks_study.py)

## Round 5 calibration stress test

The round-five extension uses 12 fresh factorial draws beginning at 496000. For each realization of the eight cells it changes the ordinary provider's slope on the latent signal to 1.3, 1.8 or 2.3. It preserves the scene law, noise realizations, specialist region, outcomes and true event probability. At slope 1.8 the ordinary provider's *noise-free logit* equals the true event logit. Actual providers still receive noise, so this setting does not force their probabilities, an arithmetic average, or a fitted tree to be calibrated. For the specialist, the slope effect is attenuated by its existing interpolation weight in the active region.

The earlier grouped and finite-population controls use 12 fresh seeds each from 496100 and 496200, respectively. The 30-family suite uses three from 496400. These comparisons retain their own default settings and are evaluated once per seed. The 288 paired factorial conditions, 48 controls and 90 fixed-suite conditions give 426 in round 5 and 1,302 across the five rounds.
