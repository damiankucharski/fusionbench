# Graded validation-to-test transfer

Frozen before scoring the evaluation banks on 24 November 2026. Does choosing one native OKAPI tree under mild, label-preserving validation disturbances improve its average Brier when the providers change at deployment?

## Prediction banks

Use all 19 default scenarios in their registered order, eight replicates, 640 development examples and 2,048 independent test examples per condition. Data seed is 1,700,000 + 1,000 × registered scenario index + replicate (0 through 7). Nested uniform masks corrupt a fraction in expectation of 0, .15 or .30 of the development labels, replacing them uniformly with an incorrect class. Both the discovery and the validation portions use these labels. Test labels remain as generated. Report uncertainty conditional on these fixed scenarios. Do not tune on their results.

Within every scenario cross the three source noise levels with one clean test and two types of deployment disturbance at .25, .50 and 1.0. A provider fault breaks the association between one randomly chosen provider and the example on a nested fraction of rows by substituting that provider's vector from another row. Calibration noise adds a fixed draw of Gaussian noise scaled by 1.5 × severity to provider log probabilities before normalization. In nine scenarios also mix a nested fraction of complete examples from the original, prespecified scenario-specific alternative generator. The endpoint is the original alternative process. The data, masks, directions of perturbation and sample order are shared across methods. Each test family is scored separately.

## Methods and controls

Use last round's unchanged single-tree selection procedure and its 448/192 discovery/validation partition. Add one candidate made by halving only the weights of the augmented head's evolved subtrees. Keep raw provider weights and the intercept fixed. For the robust selector, score each of the four pre-refit candidates on three views of the same validation rows and labels: unmodified inputs, a deterministic random provider value substituted from another row on half the examples (provider chosen separately for each row), and temperature distortion (a fixed class-log-probability multiplier of 0.5 or 2 per provider). These source-side probes do not use the test banks or the identity of the failing provider. Keep candidates within .01 of the best clean Brier skill (equivalent to .02 in full Brier), then choose the highest minimum skill on the three views. Break ties by higher clean score then by insertion order: flat, augmented, GP, halved. Both tested policies deploy one frozen native tree per bank. Training sees no test predictions or labels.

Fit the ranked-prefix Top-N selected by full-class Brier, the two standard logistic stackers with C=1 and the unweighted mean on the same prediction banks. Also measure the four native candidates individually. Logistic stackers train by penalized log loss. Prediction quality uses full-class Brier everywhere, with smaller values better.

## Endpoints

Primary contrast is new minus previous OKAPI on the six disturbed conditions available for every family, averaging the three noise levels, both disturbance types, three nonzero severities and all 19 scenarios equally. Require a two-sided 95% paired t interval across eight replicate macro-averages entirely below zero, and an upper paired bound below +.005 Brier on clean tests with clean development labels, to claim a robustness improvement. The nine scenario-specific shifts, stronger endpoints, baselines and noise curves are secondary. Do not re-select the selector, margin or primary endpoint after evaluation.

Measure validation-to-test transfer directly on the same four frozen discovery trees, before full-data refitting. For each condition report the excess test Brier of the clean-validation winner over the best of the four on test, and the fraction whose validation winner also ties for the lowest test loss (absolute tolerance 1e-12). Compare clean and perturbed conditions at fixed source noise. These diagnostic oracles use test labels only for retrospective scoring. Separately evaluate both actual deployed policies after refitting. Archive raw per-bank scores, original candidate losses on validation and test, method choices, compute costs and sample trees.
