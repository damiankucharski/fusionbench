# Replacing the deployed tree while keeping unconfirmed trees available to breed

This experiment is a user-requested bounded run. Six generations each offer twelve native crossover/mutation children, counting duplicate or unchanged offers at full price. All four core arms share one discovery set, the search seed, a maximum of eight live breeders plus one incumbent, and native survivor selection. Start with distinct provider singletons up to capacity, plus the prespecified mean as a founder when requested by the controlled studies. Keep the incumbent in a separate logical slot. Verification/research records may retain trees but are unavailable for search. Ordinary deploys the best raw discovery survivor of its final breeding population. Open/raw and open/KL allow all offers to compete for breeding and compare all offers to the incumbent as it stood before this generation. Strict/KL allows only offers with positive certified gain to compete for breeding alongside existing parents. Certified arms deploy the eligible offer with the largest lower bound or retain the incumbent. The fixed raw arm uses the largest strictly positive empirical gain. Commitments to a statistical replacement take place on previously unseen independent blocks, once per generation. No confirmation label goes into discovery fitness. Each objective block is shared across all methods for paired evaluation. Open arms have the same proposal path. Distinct evaluation and unique-genotype counts will be reported.

The simultaneous near-term decision uses full-class Brier. For each case and proposed tree, enumerate the gain over the incumbent under every possible class label. Subtract the minimum possible gain on each row, normalize by the largest row range among active rows, then invert a one-sided Bernoulli KL bound on the mean normalized gain. For independent outcomes conditional on these inputs, the Bernoulli moment bound for a [0,1] variable and AM–GM give a valid lower confidence bound. Restore the deterministic offset and scale. Charge every one of the twelve offered slots, including repeated trees, at each of six stages of a total 0.05 error budget. Condition on the history before the fresh block; the guarantee concerns label noise on those observed inputs. No protection against a new feature distribution is assumed. Separate test and oracle outputs only measure consequences.

Prespecified comparisons and seeds are documented in the [controlled](controlled/protocol.md), [neutral-intermediate XOR](barrier/protocol.md), and [full regression panel](panel/protocol.md) protocols. The primary goal is protection against harmful replacements without suppressing real nonlinear improvements. Compare the open and strict certified variants for exploration, and use the same open path with ordinary empirical promotion for the contribution of the certificate. The panel also includes unsplit native GP to account for the cost of independent blocks and the three requested fusion baselines. The additional two-stage demonstration supplies a path to measure policy behavior independently of mutation frequency. These comparisons do not change NSGA or the default production search.
