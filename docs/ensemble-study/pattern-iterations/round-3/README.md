# Round 3 · independent confirmation

For the [frozen comparison](../round-3-protocol.md), each of the 19 fixed scenarios contributed ten fresh development/test draws to the primary analysis. Joint Brier fell from 0.21524 to 0.20673. The paired 95% CI for the change was −0.01079 to −0.00624. Clean Brier fell from 0.18433 to 0.17823 (change −0.00611, CI −0.00790 to −0.00431). Both gates passed. On the primary endpoint 18 of the 19 family point estimates improved. Parity regressed by 0.00141. Family-level signs are descriptive. Probability stacking scored 0.24268, the ranked-prefix mean 0.34259 and the unweighted mean 0.38373. Even the original native reference outperformed these controls in the joint average. Probability stacking still beat the new procedure on five individual joint family means.

For the withheld fault, two correlated providers simultaneously supplied their predictions for the same different row on about half of test inputs. Pair selection used development predictions only. Brier improved from 0.31975 to 0.29914 (−0.02061, CI −0.02775 to −0.01346). On nine canonical distribution shifts, the challenger scored 0.30359 against 0.31692 for the incumbent and 0.30344 for probability stacking. The paired difference against stacking was +0.00015 (CI −0.00428 to +0.00457). These secondary contrasts were prespecified, with nominal intervals and no multiplicity adjustment.

Validation chose a different candidate family on 40 of 190 primary banks; the augmented head was deployed 112 times by the challenger and 93 times by the incumbent. Across 38 archived paired refits (19 development banks, two search seeds), the mean absolute coefficient on shared raw inputs of the flat head fell in 30 and tied in eight. This comparison fixes the flat architecture. Within the full procedure, weight fitting also changes subsequent subtree screening and validation selection. These data do not isolate what fraction of the performance gain came from each change.

On 38 banks with identical data and a second search seed, the mean absolute change in joint Brier was 0.01142 for original OKAPI and 0.01083 for the challenger. The largest change rose from 0.08340 to 0.09322. With only two search seeds per bank this is a descriptive sensitivity check. Both methods still produced substantially different outcomes on some banks.

Four-worker panel runtime was 118.92 s. On the 190 primary fits, the regularized arm used 5,805 scalar score calls per fit on average, versus 5,778 for the original. Under this shared CPU run mean fit times were 0.93 and 0.98 s, respectively. Probability stacking averaged 0.0045 s. These elapsed times include different estimator overheads and are specific to this environment. The external [digits check](../digits/README.md) reports a separate result.
